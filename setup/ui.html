Kid Planner Setup

Installs the planner for your Kindle. It takes about 5 minutes.

Before you start

Tested on Kindle Paperwhite 3 (7th generation, 2015). Other touch Kindles can work, but they are not tested yet.

  1. Prepare your Kindle (once, about 30 to 60 minutes). Jailbreak the Kindle and install KUAL. A jailbreak lets the Kindle run programs that Amazon did not make. KUAL is the menu where Kid Planner will appear. Setup does most of it for you: click Prepare my Kindle below. To do it by hand, follow the step-by-step guide: Prepare your Kindle. You are done when your Kindle library shows a book called "KUAL".
  2. You have a free Cloudflare account. The planner runs there. If you do not have one, sign up here and confirm your email.

Prepare your Kindle

Setup puts the jailbreak WinterBreak and the menu KUAL on the Kindle. You tap a few things on the Kindle. It takes about 30 minutes.

A jailbreak can fail, and a failed jailbreak can make the Kindle unusable. Do not do a factory reset during or after the jailbreak.

Setup copies the books from the Kindle to your Documents folder before it changes anything. How to remove a screen password, and how to back up by hand: Backup and screen password.

Another jailbreak (not WinterBreak)? Do the jailbreak by hand with kindlemodding.org, connect the Kindle, then click Install KUAL below.

  1. On the Kindle, open Settings, then Device Options, then Device Info. If the firmware version is 5.18.1 or newer, stop here: WinterBreak does not work on it. Check kindlemodding.org for other methods.
  2. Make sure that the Kindle is registered to an Amazon account and has no screen password.
  3. Turn on Airplane Mode, then restart the Kindle.
  4. Connect the Kindle to this computer with its USB cable.

No Kindle connected.

Setup filled the Kindle storage, so the Kindle cannot download an Amazon update. On the Kindle:

  1. Eject the Kindle on this computer, then unplug the cable.
  2. On the home screen, tap the cart icon to open the Kindle Store. If the Kindle asks to turn off Airplane Mode, tap Yes.
  3. A page called Mesquito opens. Tap the WinterBreak icon.
  4. Wait about 30 seconds. If lines of text fill the screen and the home screen restarts, the jailbreak worked. If no text appears, do steps 2 and 3 again.
  5. Connect the Kindle to this computer again, then click Install KUAL.

Only after the jailbreak worked. Setup removes the filler files and the update files, then copies KUAL.

The planner is installed

On the Kindle:

  1. Eject the Kindle on this computer, then unplug the cable.
  2. Open KUAL on the Kindle.
  3. Tap Kid Dashboard, then Start dashboard. From now on it also starts by itself when the Kindle turns on.
