Kid Planner Setup

Installs the planner for your Kindle. It takes about 5 minutes.

Before you start

  1. Your Kindle is jailbroken and has KUAL. This step is not automatic, because it depends on your Kindle. Follow the guide at kindlemodding.org.
  2. You have a free Cloudflare account. The planner runs there. If you do not have one, sign up here and confirm your email.

Your planner

Shown on the Kindle lock screen and in the dashboard.

At least 10 characters. Write it down: you need it to open the dashboard.


Copy the planner to the Kindle

Connect the Kindle to this computer with its USB cable. Setup finds it by itself.

The planner is installed

On the Kindle:

  1. Eject the Kindle on this computer, then unplug the cable.
  2. Open KUAL on the Kindle.
  3. Tap Kid Dashboard, then Start dashboard. From now on it also starts by itself when the Kindle turns on.
